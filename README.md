# 1. Project Title / Headline

## Uber Analytics Dashboard: Ride Booking, Revenue and Customer Insights

A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Uber ride operations, focusing on booking performance, revenue trends, customer behavior, cancellations, vehicle-wise contributions, and location-based demand patterns.

---

# 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Uber Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help users explore and monitor ride bookings, revenue generation, ride distances, customer ratings, and operational efficiency. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Uber's business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

This tool is intended for business analysts, operations managers, transportation service providers, and data enthusiasts who seek to understand booking trends, customer behavior, and revenue patterns.

---

# 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
* **Power Query** – Data transformation and cleaning layer used for preparing the dataset.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for calculated measures, KPIs, and dynamic reporting.
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships established among tables to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
* **Microsoft Excel** – Source dataset used for analysis.
* **File Formats**

  * `.pbit` for dashboard development.
  * `.xlsx` for source data.
  * `.png` for dashboard previews.

---

# 4. Data Source

### Source: Uber Ride Booking Dataset (Excel)

The dataset contains ride-level information related to Uber operations and customer activity.

It includes details such as:

* Booking IDs and booking status.
* Pickup and drop locations.
* Vehicle categories.
* Ride distance and booking value.
* Customer and driver ratings.
* Payment methods.
* Customer and driver cancellation reasons.
* Incomplete ride reasons.

The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query and DAX to support interactive analysis and business reporting.

---

# 5. Features / Highlights

## • Business Problem

Ride-sharing platforms generate large amounts of operational data every day, making it difficult to monitor performance and extract meaningful insights from raw records.

Key questions such as:

* Which vehicle categories generate the highest revenue?
* How do bookings and revenue vary over time?
* What factors contribute to ride cancellations?
* Which locations experience the highest demand?
* How satisfied are customers and drivers?
* What are the peak hours and busiest regions?

are difficult to answer efficiently without proper visualization and analysis.

---

## • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive analytical tool that:

* Monitors overall booking performance.
* Tracks revenue and ride trends over time.
* Identifies cancellation and incomplete ride patterns.
* Evaluates customer and driver satisfaction.
* Analyzes vehicle category contributions.
* Supports operational and strategic decision-making.

---

## • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

### Key KPIs

The dashboard provides a high-level overview through important metrics:

* Total Bookings
* Lost Bookings
* Total Revenue
* Total Distance Covered
* Average Ride Distance

These KPIs help monitor the overall health and performance of the business.

---

### Vehicle Filter Panel

An interactive filter enables users to analyze the dashboard for specific vehicle categories such as:

* Auto
* Bike
* Go Mini
* Go Sedan
* Premier Sedan
* Uber XL

---

### Booking Trend Analysis

A line chart visualizes completed bookings over different months, helping identify seasonal patterns and demand fluctuations.

---

### Revenue Trend Analysis

Column charts display monthly and quarterly revenue performance, helping understand business growth and high-performing periods.

---

### Revenue by Vehicle Type

A horizontal bar chart compares revenue generated by each vehicle category, making it easier to identify the most profitable segments.

---

### Ride Status Analysis

Circular KPI visuals display:

* Completed Rides
* Cancelled Rides
* Incomplete Rides

These metrics provide insights into operational efficiency and service reliability.

---

### Top Pickup and Drop Locations

The dashboard highlights high-demand pickup and drop areas based on booking counts.

This helps understand geographical demand patterns and supports better fleet allocation.

---

### Customer and Driver Ratings

Average rider and driver ratings provide insights into customer satisfaction and service quality.

---

### Location Analysis

Location-based visuals help analyze:

* Total distance covered.
* Busy regions.
* Peak time slots.
* Vehicle-wise distance distribution.

---

### Rider Analysis

Dedicated visuals provide insights into:

* Ride cancellation reasons.
* Payment methods.
* First-time riders.
* Returning riders.
* Regular riders.
